# Aakar Co-op Housing Society app — help guide

The HELP assistant answers only from this guide. Keep answers short and step-by-step.
If the answer is not here, or something looks broken, say so and point to the app coordinator.
Never ask for or repeat passwords, OTPs, bank details or card numbers.

## About the app
- The app of Aakar Co-op Housing Society, Sukh Tower (Mirambica Road, Naranpura, Ahmedabad). 91 flats in Towers A, B and C.
- Open it at aakarsociety.vercel.app on a phone or computer. It can be added to the phone's home screen (INSTALL APP button on Android / Chrome; on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen).
- Two kinds of login: a **flat login** for each flat (members), and **committee logins** for society committee members.

## Logging in and passwords
- Log in with your flat number (for example A-101) and your password. Committee members log in with their email.
- No password yet, or forgot it? Ask the society committee. They create a new one-time password for the flat; you set your own password at the next login.
- The eye button in the password box shows the password for 20 seconds.
- To change your password: tap **PASSWORD** at the top of the screen, enter the new password twice, SAVE PASSWORD.
- To log out: the gold button at the top right.
- Members cannot sign up by themselves; logins are created only by the committee.

## Finding your way
- Tabs across the top: COVER, INDEX, FLOOR, MY FLAT (called DETAIL in committee logins), BUSINESS, DIRECTORY, NOTICES, CONTACTS, EVENTS, VAULT. Committee logins also have BILLING, COMMITTEE, ADMIN and REPORTS.
- **On a phone** (since 30 Oct 2026) the app opens on a **home screen of buttons** instead of tabs. Tap a button to open just that function; tap the gold **HOME** button at the top left (or the phone's back button) to come back to the buttons.
  - Owner (flat login): your flat number, a dark card with the amount OUTSTANDING and **I HAVE PAID** / **VIEW BILL**, then BILLS & LEDGER, MY FAMILY, MY VEHICLES, DOCUMENTS, MY LETTERS, COMPLAINTS, BOOK PLOT, NOMINEE (not for a flat given on rent), EMERGENCY, WHO TO CONTACT, MY DETAILS (edit your details, print your card) and ACTION POINTS (only when you have open ones). Under SOCIETY: the screens the committee has opened for members (NOTICES, EVENTS, CONTACTS, DIRECTORY …).
  - Tenant login: MY FAMILY, MY VEHICLES, EMERGENCY, COMPLAINTS, BOOK PLOT, WHO TO CONTACT, and under SOCIETY: NOTICES, EVENTS, CONTACTS.
  - Committee login: BILLING, COMMITTEE, FLATS, REPORTS, ADMIN, MY FLAT (when the login is linked to a flat), and the society screens. Each opens the full screen of that tab.
  - PASSWORD and LOG OUT are at the top of the home screen. On a computer (or a phone turned sideways on a wide screen) the tabs are shown as before.
- On a computer the row of tabs is across the top.
- The committee chooses which tabs members see (ADMIN → MEMBER SCREENS). MY FLAT and NOTICES are always there; if another tab is missing, the committee has turned it off for members.
- HELP: on a phone it is the round **?** button at the bottom right; on a computer the HELP button.
- The left panel (on a computer) shows Tower A, B, C and the COMMITTEE with FILTER links, and the SOCIETY NOTICE.

## What each tab shows
- **COVER**: the society's record page: name, registration number and date, address, the SOCIETY SEAL box (91 flats: Tower A 40, Tower B 40, Tower C 11), ledger status, the committee members with their flats (Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, then Committee Members, then Co-opted Members; within each, by flat number), short notes (terrace logic, vault), quick links to the other tabs, and WHO TO CONTACT (the committee member responsible for each area, like lift or water).
- **INDEX**: a grid of all flats in Towers A, B and C, with owner names. Use the tower FILTER to see one tower. Members tap their own flat to open MY FLAT; committee logins can open any flat. Flats given on rent show ON RENT. Tower C homes are the 4 BHK homes of the B building and carry two numbers, for example B-201/202; log in with either number (B201 or B202). Exception: B-101 and B-102 are two separate flats (each with its own owner, maintenance bill and login), also listed under Tower C.
- **FLOOR**: floor plans tower-wise. Shows PLAN READY or NO PLAN UPLOADED. Committee can upload a plan with UPLOAD FLOOR PLAN PDF/DWG. Top-floor flats of Tower A (A-1001 to A-1004) and the Tower C home B-1001/1002 own the terrace (TERRACE LOGIC). Tower B's top floor (B-1003 to B-1006) has no terrace and pays the same as the other Tower B flats.
- **MY FLAT**: everything about your own flat (details below).
- **BUSINESS**: the society business directory: members' professions and businesses with flat, office address and phone. HOW TO GET LISTED: open MY FLAT → FAMILY MEMBERS → Edit (or + ADD MEMBER) and fill in Profession / Business, office address and office phone; it appears in BUSINESS automatically.
- **DIRECTORY**: MEMBER DIRECTORY of families and mobile numbers, only for flats that gave consent (others show only flat number and owner or tenant name). Also a COMMITTEE DIRECTORY. PRINT / SAVE AS PDF prints it.
- **NOTICES**: society notices (see Notices and banners).
- **EVENTS**: events, photos, registration and passes (see Events).
- **CONTACTS**: important phone numbers for the society (lift, fire, security, MyGate, CCTV, electricity, Torrent Power, borewell, garbage collection, plumber …), grouped by category. Tap a number to call. Search by name or number. Below the list: WHO TO CONTACT (the committee member responsible for each area). Committee logins add contacts with + ADD CONTACT, edit them, and switch off a contact that is no longer used (it is kept, not deleted, and can be switched on again).
- **VAULT**: SOCIETY DOCUMENT VAULT with the society's official documents (for example Registration Certificate, Society Bye-laws, Terrace Ownership Agreement marked IMPORTANT, Building Plan Approval, Fire NOC, Lift AMC) and OTHER SOCIETY DOCUMENTS. Tap VIEW to open a document. Only the committee can UPLOAD, REPLACE or DELETE.
- Committee only: **BILLING** (bills, receipts, VERIFY PAYMENTS, dues, letters, settings), **COMMITTEE** (COMPLAINTS, BOOKINGS, RESPONSIBILITIES, AMC CONTRACTS, MINUTES), **ADMIN** (logins, MEMBER SCREENS, backup, test tools, HELP GUIDE, storage), **REPORTS** (flat-wise reports: PDF REPORT, CSV / EXCEL, FLAT DOCUMENTS, NOMINATIONS, FLAT PROFILE CARDS).

## MY FLAT in detail
- Top of the page: flat number, tower, floor, type, owner, and QUICK ACTIONS: VIEW FLOOR, PRINT MY CARD (a one-page flat profile card), EDIT MY DETAILS, MY LEDGER ↓, MY VEHICLES ↓, MY LETTERS ↓, WHO TO CONTACT ↓, COMPLAINTS ↓, BOOK COMMON PLOT ↓, NOMINEE ↓ (the ↓ buttons jump down the page to that section).
- **EDIT MY DETAILS**: update your flat's details shown on the page (such as contact details), then SAVE TO LEDGER.
  - You can change your mobile, email and address any time.
  - If the owner name (or tenant name) is empty, you can fill it in once. After that only the committee can change it, because it is printed on bills and letters. Ask the committee if it is wrong.
  - Owner / tenant (who lives in the flat) is changed by the committee. Tell the committee when the flat is given on rent or the tenant leaves.
- **FAMILY MEMBERS**: the people living in the flat. + ADD MEMBER to add someone, Edit to change, then SAVE MEMBER. A committee login can mark each family member who is on the society committee: Edit → ★ On the society committee → designation. Two members of one family can both be on it (for example Committee Member and Co-opted Member); committee lists then show each person with their own designation. The **head of family** can be added once; after that only the committee can change the head's name or who is head (the head's mobile, email and profession you can still edit). Each member can have a Profession / Business (shown in the BUSINESS directory).
- **VEHICLES**: + ADD VEHICLE with type and number (for example GJ 01 AB 1234).
- **EMERGENCY CONTACTS**: + ADD CONTACT for people outside the society to call if something happens. Seen only by your flat and the committee. Please add at least one.
- **MEMBER DIRECTORY CONSENT**: tick "Share our family details with society members" to appear in DIRECTORY. Without it, only flat number and owner (or tenant) name are shown. You can change it any time.
- **FLAT DOCUMENTS**: the documents the society needs from each flat. First choose **Which document?** from the list — Index, Dastavej, Torrent Power bill, Property tax bill, Rent agreement (only for rented flats), Share certificate, Allotment letter, Prathmik karar, or Other (type the name) — then UPLOAD DOCUMENT and pick the file.
  - Photos and scanned PDFs are made smaller before upload; the app shows the result, for example "Compressed 4.2 MB → 0.6 MB". A compressed PDF is saved as pictures, so its text can no longer be selected; that is normal.
  - The checklist below the button shows ✓ for documents uploaded and ○ for documents still to upload.
  - Still over 4 MB after compressing: scan at a lower setting, or split the document into parts.
  - Seen only by your flat and the committee.
- **MY BILLS & RECEIPTS** and **MY LETTERS**: your bills, receipts, ledger and letters from the society.
- **MY ACTION POINTS**: tasks given to you in committee meeting minutes, with a link to view the minutes.
- Rented flats: the tenant's family, vehicles and rent agreement date are recorded; the owner is marked "does not live here".

## Complaints
- MY FLAT → **COMPLAINTS ↓** → **+ RAISE A COMPLAINT**.
  1. PROBLEM AREA: choose the area (for example Lift, Water). The app shows which committee member it goes to.
  2. WHAT IS THE PROBLEM?: a few lines.
  3. PHOTO (optional): 📷 ADD PHOTO. It is made smaller before upload.
  4. SEND COMPLAINT. You get a complaint number like CMP/26-27/0001. If the area has a phone number, 📲 ALSO TELL ON WHATSAPP sends a ready message to that member (you press send in WhatsApp).
- MY COMPLAINTS shows each complaint with its status: **OPEN** → **IN PROGRESS** (with an update) → **CLOSED** (with what was done). HISTORY shows every step.
- Not resolved after it was closed? Raise it again and mention the old complaint number.
- Committee members: complaints for your area appear in your own MY FLAT under **ASSIGNED TO YOU**, and every complaint is in COMMITTEE → **COMPLAINTS** (the red number = open complaints). IN PROGRESS (optional note), CLOSE ✓ (write what was done — the member sees it), REOPEN and CHANGE AREA (committee logins only), 📲 TELL MEMBER, PRINT.
- Only your flat, the responsible committee member(s) and the committee see a complaint.

## Booking the common plot
- Areas: **TOWER-A**, **TOWER-B** and **COMMON**. Each can be booked for a date, **MORNING** or **EVENING**. No charge is taken in the app.
- MY FLAT → **BOOK COMMON PLOT ↓** → **+ BOOK AN AREA**.
  1. DATE: from tomorrow up to 90 days ahead.
  2. TIME: MORNING or EVENING.
  3. AREA: tick one or more. Areas already booked or requested for that date and time are greyed out, with the flat number. SELECT ALL FREE AREAS ticks every free area.
  4. PURPOSE (optional), then SEND REQUEST.
- The request goes to the **Chairman and the Secretary**; approval by either one is enough (they approve from their own committee login). MY BOOKINGS shows WAITING FOR APPROVAL, then **CONFIRMED**, or NOT APPROVED with the reason.
- When a booking is confirmed, the app posts a notice and a login banner for everyone: "<area> is booked by Flat … on … (morning/evening). Please remove vehicles parked in <area>." If all three areas are booked: "All vehicles should be parked outside the premises."
- **Cancel**: MY BOOKINGS → CANCEL BOOKING, up to the day before. On the day itself, ask the committee. The notice then shows CANCELLED and the banner goes off.
- Chairman / Secretary: log in with your committee (email) login. Requests appear in your MY FLAT under REQUESTS TO APPROVE, and in COMMITTEE → **BOOKINGS** (WAITING, CONFIRMED, PAST). APPROVE ✓, or NOT APPROVE with a reason. 📲 SHARE NOTICE sends the notice text to the society WhatsApp group; PRINT LIST prints confirmed bookings (for the security guard).

## Nominee
- MY FLAT → **NOMINEE ↓** → **+ ADD NOMINEE**: the person(s) the flat's share and interest go to. The nominee need not live in the society.
- Fill in: full name, relation, date of birth, **Aadhaar – last 4 digits only**, PAN, **share %**, and mobile / address if they live elsewhere.
- The app keeps only the last 4 digits of Aadhaar. If you type the full number, it keeps just the last 4. The full number is written by hand on the paper form if the society needs it.
- More than one nominee is allowed; the shares must add up to **100%** (the app shows how much is left).
- Nominee under 18: the guardian's name is needed.
- **PRINT FORM TO SIGN** (once the shares add up to 100%): sign it with two witnesses and give it to the society office. The committee then ticks "signed form received". Changing a nominee later needs a new signed form.
- Seen only by the flat's owner and the committee. In a rented flat the tenant's login does not show the owner's nominee; the owner gives the details to the committee.

## Tenant login (rented flats)
- The flat login (flat number, e.g. A-101) is the OWNER's. A rented flat can also have a separate TENANT login: the flat number followed by T, e.g. **A-101-T**. The committee creates it on the flat's page (TENANT LOGIN → CREATE TENANT LOGIN) and gives the first password; the tenant sets their own at first login.
- The tenant sees MY FLAT (family members, vehicles, emergency contacts of the flat, complaints, BOOK COMMON PLOT, who to contact), NOTICES, CONTACTS and EVENTS (and COVER if shown to members).
- The tenant does not see bills, receipts, the ledger, "I have paid", nominees, letters or the flat's documents; those stay with the owner.
- While the flat has a tenant login, the owner's login can see the family, vehicles and emergency contacts but not change them (the tenant keeps them up to date).
- When the committee marks TENANT MOVED OUT, the tenant login stops working at once. The committee can also RESET the tenant's password or REVOKE the login; the family list, vehicles and complaints are kept.

## Letters from the society (requests)
- Members (owner's flat login) ask in MY FLAT → REQUEST A LETTER: choose the letter from the society's standard formats (NOC for bank loan, NOC for sale, NOC for renting / police verification, address proof, no-dues certificate, membership letter …) or "Other", who it is addressed to, and the purpose. Tenant logins cannot ask for letters.
- The request shows WAITING, then APPROVED • BEING PREPARED, then ISSUED (or NOT ISSUED with the reason). The issued letter is in MY LETTERS (view, print, share PDF).
- Letters for a bank are issued as an UNSIGNED copy: the Chairman or Secretary signs and stamps the paper letter by hand. Until it is handed over MY LETTERS shows READY TO COLLECT; after that, "collected" with the date.
- Committee: BILLING → LETTERS. The Chairman or the Secretary (from their own committee login) APPROVES or declines a request (NOT ISSUE, with a reason); then ISSUE LETTER opens a draft filled from the format and the flat's records (owner name, flat, share certificate, member since, dues …) to check and edit before ISSUE & PRINT. For an unsigned bank letter, tick SIGNED COPY HANDED OVER on the flat's letters list when it has been given. LETTER FORMATS: add or edit the society's formats (fields such as {{owner_name}}, {{flat_no}}, {{dues}}), switch a format off; formats are never deleted.

## Bills, receipts and paying maintenance
- Maintenance rates are set per year and billed every half-year (April and October): each bill is half of the yearly rate. A flat given on rent pays 1.5 times the standard maintenance; the bill is still in the owner's name and shows the extra amount on its own line.
- Your bills and receipts are in MY FLAT → MY BILLS & RECEIPTS, with the balance due and a MEMBER LEDGER CARD. PRINT MY LEDGER and STATEMENT PDF give printable copies.
- Pay by UPI / bank transfer or cheque using the details printed on the bill. Write your flat number in the payment remark.
- After paying, tell the committee: **I HAVE PAID** in MY BILLS & RECEIPTS.
  1. Choose the bill you paid.
  2. Upload the payment screenshot (Google Pay, PhonePe, bank app…). Amount, date, UTR and bank are filled in from it — check them.
  3. Paid by cash or cheque, or no screenshot? Use "Fill in by hand".
  4. Press SEND TO COMMITTEE.
- The committee checks the bank and makes the receipt. Your dues reduce only after they verify it. Status shows WAITING FOR VERIFICATION, then VERIFIED • RECEIPT MADE.
- Part payment is allowed; the app will point it out.
- If the app says the reference was already reported, check you are not reporting the same payment twice.
- "Could not read the screenshot": fill in the details by hand; the screenshot is still attached for the committee.

## Notices and banners
- NOTICES tab lists society notices, newest first, in English or Gujarati as typed by the committee.
- Some notices appear as a large banner after login. ✕ closes it for this visit; it shows again at the next login until the committee turns it off or its end date passes. READ NOTICE opens the full text.
- Closed the banner and want to see it again? NOTICES → **SEE LOGIN BANNERS NOW** (shown when a banner is on), or log out and in again.
- Common plot booking notices are posted automatically when a booking is confirmed, and show as a banner until the booking date.
- Committee: publish from NOTICES → NEW NOTICE (choose tower, attach a poster if needed). PRINT / PDF prints the notice on the society letterhead; choose who signs in "PRINTED NOTICES SIGNED BY".
- Committee: the banner switch on each notice shows its state — **BANNER OFF • TURN ON** (not a banner now; tap to show it after login) or **★ BANNER ON • TURN OFF** (shown after login; tap to stop).

## Events, registration and passes
- EVENTS tab: upcoming, ongoing and past events, with PHOTOS, TEAM (organising committee), MINUTES and ACCOUNTS.
- When registration is open, a gold **REGISTER →** card appears at the top of EVENTS.
- To register:
  1. Open the event → REGISTRATION.
  2. For each meal, use + / − for Members (Regular, Jain) and Guests (Regular, Jain). The numbers start with the people living in your flat.
  3. Check the amount to pay and press SEND REGISTRATION.
  4. You can change the numbers until the last date, as long as you have not sent the payment yet. Set all numbers to 0 and save to cancel.
- To pay (paid meals): choose **UPI / BANK** (pay to the event's UPI ID shown, upload the screenshot, check the details, SEND TO EVENT TREASURER) or **CASH** (choose the event team member you gave the cash to, enter amount and date, SEND).
- Your payment shows "waiting for the event team to confirm" until the convenor / treasurer confirms it (UPI) or the person who received the cash confirms it. If rejected, the reason is shown — check and send again.
- **Passes**: once payment is confirmed (or straight away for free meals), YOUR PASSES shows one pass per meal with a QR code, your flat number and the counts. SHARE PASS sends it on WhatsApp. Family members coming at different times can use the same pass. Show it at the gate: the event team scans it and counts how many come in, so a pass can be used for at most the number on it.
- Event team (convenor / treasurer): REGISTRATION SETTINGS sets meals, prices, Jain option, last date and UPI ID; PAYMENTS TO CONFIRM; totals per meal; CATERER COUNT (PDF); ENTER OR CHANGE FOR A FLAT for door-to-door collection. Confirmed payments are added to the event ACCOUNTS automatically.
- Event team roles: **Convenor** (runs the event, chosen by the committee), **Treasurer**, **Member** and **Assisting** (helps on the day). The convenor adds people in TEAM: a flat (the owner's household) or the **tenant of a rented flat** (only as Assisting, and only if the tenant has a tenant login). Assisting sees the team, the event's minutes and the registration / gate list, and can scan passes at the gate, but not the event's money book (ACCOUNTS) and cannot receive cash.
- **At the gate** (every event team member): REGISTRATION → **📷 SCAN PASSES AT GATE**. Hold the pass's QR code in the square; the screen shows the flat, the meal, PASS FOR / ALREADY IN / LEFT. Set how many are coming in now and tap **✓ LET IN**. A full pass shows PASS FULL; a pass not yet paid shows Payment not confirmed. No QR (phone not with them)? Tap **FIND FLAT**, choose the flat and the meal. A wrong entry can be undone in LAST ENTRIES (by the person who made it within 15 minutes, or by the convenor / treasurer). The camera needs permission the first time.
- After scanning starts, the totals and the CATERER COUNT (PDF) show **ATTENDED** next to the registered numbers, per meal and per flat.
- Event money is kept separate from society maintenance bills.

## Directory, business and documents
- VAULT holds the society's documents (registration certificate, bye-laws, plans, NOC, AMCs). Flat documents (sale deed, share certificate) are in MY FLAT and are visible only to your flat and the committee.
- Maximum file size 4 MB (after compressing, for flat documents); PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX, XLSX.
- Files open through a private link made for you when you tap VIEW, SIGNED COPY or a picture. The link works for a few minutes only, so a copied or forwarded link stops working. To open the file again, tap it again in the app.

## Meetings and minutes
- AGM / SGM minutes are visible to all members once the committee marks them FINAL (NOTICES → MINUTES OF GENERAL MEETINGS: READ MINUTES and SIGNED COPY). Committee meeting minutes are for committee members only; a committee member's own flat login also shows them under COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES.
- An AGM announcement in NOTICES is a notice, not the minutes; the minutes appear separately once final.
- If you were given an action point in a meeting, it appears in MY ACTION POINTS with a link to view the minutes.

## Common problems
- **Something new is missing or the screen looks old**: reload the page (pull down on phone, or Ctrl + Shift + R on a computer). On the installed phone app, close it fully from the app switcher and open it again.
- **A tab is missing**: on a phone, swipe the tab row left. Some tabs are only for committee logins.
- **Upload is stuck on an office laptop**: some office laptops block web pages from reading files. Use your phone, or a personal computer.
- **A page or tab opens blank**: reload; if it stays blank, tell the app coordinator which button you pressed.
- **"This link has expired" or a file does not open from a forwarded link**: file links work for a few minutes only. Open the app and tap the file again.
- **"This file is not available to your login"**: the file belongs to another flat or to the committee. Ask the committee if you think you should see it.
- **Error message on screen**: take a screenshot of the message and send it to the app coordinator.
- **The site does not open at all**: check your internet; if it still does not open, it may be a hosting problem — tell the app coordinator.

## Who to contact
- App questions not answered here, and anything that looks broken: the **app coordinator** of the society committee (name and WhatsApp number set by the committee).
- Bills, payments and receipts: the Treasurer. Notices and society matters: the Secretary. The "Who to contact" list on the COVER page shows the committee member responsible for each area, with the backup members below. A complaint goes to the responsible member and all backups of its area.
- Committee → RESPONSIBILITIES: each area has one responsible member and backups (up to 4 by default). The Chairman or the Secretary, from their own committee login, chooses the members; the main admin login adds, renames, hides or reorders areas and sets the maximum number of backups. The responsible member cannot also be a backup of the same area, and only committee members can be chosen. When someone leaves the committee, their areas show "not on committee — choose someone else" until replaced. Areas are never deleted, only hidden.
