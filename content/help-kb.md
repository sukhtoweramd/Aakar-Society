# Aakar Co-op Housing Society app — help guide

The HELP assistant answers only from this guide. Keep answers short and step-by-step.
If the answer is not here, or something looks broken, say so and point to the app coordinator.
Never ask for or repeat passwords, OTPs, bank details or card numbers.

## About the app
- The app of Aakar Co-op Housing Society, Sukh Tower (Mirambica Road, Naranpura, Ahmedabad). 90 flats in Towers A, B and C.
- Open it at aakarsociety.vercel.app on a phone or computer. It can be added to the phone's home screen (INSTALL APP button on Android / Chrome; on iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen).
- Two kinds of login: a **flat login** for each flat (members), and **committee logins** for society committee members.

## Logging in and passwords
- Log in with your flat number (for example A-101) and your password. Committee members log in with their email.
- No password yet, or forgot it? Ask the society committee. They create a new one-time password for the flat; you set your own password at the next login.
- The eye button in the password box shows the password for 20 seconds.
- To change your password: tap **PASSWORD** at the top of the screen, enter the new password twice, SAVE PASSWORD.
- To log out: the gold button at the top right.
- Members cannot sign up by themselves; logins are created only by the committee.

## Finding your way
- Tabs across the top: COVER, INDEX (all flats), MY FLAT (shown as DETAIL for committee logins), DIRECTORY, NOTICES, EVENTS, BUSINESS, VAULT, FLOOR. Committee logins also have BILLING, COMMITTEE, ADMIN and REPORTS.
- On a phone the row of tabs is wider than the screen: swipe the tab row left to see more tabs.
- Your own flat: the MY FLAT tab (or tap your flat in INDEX). It shows family members, vehicles, emergency contacts, your flat documents, and MY BILLS & RECEIPTS.

## Bills, receipts and paying maintenance
- Your bills and receipts are in MY FLAT → MY BILLS & RECEIPTS, with the balance due and a MEMBER LEDGER CARD. PRINT MY LEDGER and STATEMENT PDF give printable copies.
- Pay by UPI / bank transfer or cheque using the details printed on the bill. Write your flat number in the payment remark.
- After paying, tell the committee: **I HAVE PAID** in MY BILLS & RECEIPTS.
  1. Choose the bill you paid.
  2. Upload the payment screenshot (Google Pay, PhonePe, bank app…). Amount, date, UTR and bank are filled in from it — check them.
  3. Paid by cash or cheque, or no screenshot? Use "Fill in by hand".
  4. Press SEND TO COMMITTEE.
- The committee checks the bank and makes the receipt. Your dues reduce only after they verify it. Status shows WAITING FOR VERIFICATION, then VERIFIED • RECEIPT MADE.
- Part payment is allowed; the app will point it out.
- If the app says the reference was already reported, check you are not reporting the same payment twice.
- "Could not read the screenshot": fill in the details by hand; the screenshot is still attached for the committee.

## Notices and banners
- NOTICES tab lists society notices, newest first, in English or Gujarati as typed by the committee.
- Some notices appear as a large banner after login. ✕ closes it for this visit; it shows again at the next login until the committee turns it off or its end date passes. READ NOTICE opens the full text.
- Committee: publish from NOTICES → NEW NOTICE (choose tower, attach a poster if needed). PRINT / PDF prints the notice on the society letterhead; choose who signs in "PRINTED NOTICES SIGNED BY". BANNER: ON ✕ switches a banner off.

## Events, registration and passes
- EVENTS tab: upcoming, ongoing and past events, with PHOTOS, TEAM (organising committee), MINUTES and ACCOUNTS.
- When registration is open, a gold **REGISTER →** card appears at the top of EVENTS.
- To register:
  1. Open the event → REGISTRATION.
  2. For each meal, use + / − for Members (Regular, Jain) and Guests (Regular, Jain). The numbers start with the people living in your flat.
  3. Check the amount to pay and press SEND REGISTRATION.
  4. You can change the numbers until the last date, as long as you have not sent the payment yet. Set all numbers to 0 and save to cancel.
- To pay (paid meals): choose **UPI / BANK** (pay to the event's UPI ID shown, upload the screenshot, check the details, SEND TO EVENT TREASURER) or **CASH** (choose the event team member you gave the cash to, enter amount and date, SEND).
- Your payment shows "waiting for the event team to confirm" until the convenor / treasurer confirms it (UPI) or the person who received the cash confirms it. If rejected, the reason is shown — check and send again.
- **Passes**: once payment is confirmed (or straight away for free meals), YOUR PASSES shows one pass per meal with a QR code, your flat number and the counts. SHARE PASS sends it on WhatsApp. Family members coming at different times can use the same pass. Show it at the counter.
- Event team (convenor / treasurer): REGISTRATION SETTINGS sets meals, prices, Jain option, last date and UPI ID; PAYMENTS TO CONFIRM; totals per meal; CATERER COUNT (PDF); ENTER OR CHANGE FOR A FLAT for door-to-door collection. Confirmed payments are added to the event ACCOUNTS automatically.
- Event money is kept separate from society maintenance bills.

## Directory, business and documents
- DIRECTORY shows families and mobile numbers only for flats that gave consent. You can give or withdraw consent for your flat in MY FLAT.
- BUSINESS lists members' professions and businesses.
- VAULT holds the society's documents (registration certificate, bye-laws, plans, NOC, AMCs). Flat documents (sale deed, share certificate) are in MY FLAT and are visible only to your flat and the committee.
- Maximum file size 4 MB; PDF, JPG, PNG, DOCX, XLSX.

## Meetings and minutes
- AGM / SGM minutes are visible to all members once final. Committee meeting minutes are for committee members only.
- If you were given an action point in a meeting, it appears in MY ACTION POINTS with a link to view the minutes.

## Common problems
- **Something new is missing or the screen looks old**: reload the page (pull down on phone, or Ctrl + Shift + R on a computer). On the installed phone app, close it fully from the app switcher and open it again.
- **A tab is missing**: on a phone, swipe the tab row left. Some tabs are only for committee logins.
- **Upload is stuck on an office laptop**: some office laptops block web pages from reading files. Use your phone, or a personal computer.
- **A page or tab opens blank**: reload; if it stays blank, tell the app coordinator which button you pressed.
- **Error message on screen**: take a screenshot of the message and send it to the app coordinator.
- **The site does not open at all**: check your internet; if it still does not open, it may be a hosting problem — tell the app coordinator.

## Who to contact
- App questions not answered here, and anything that looks broken: the **app coordinator** of the society committee (name and WhatsApp number set by the committee).
- Bills, payments and receipts: the Treasurer. Notices and society matters: the Secretary. The "Who to contact" list on the COVER page shows the committee member responsible for each area.
